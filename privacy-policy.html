Dignity Business Consulting (DBC) – Privacy Policy

Empowering Entrepreneurs. Elevating Excellence.

Our Commitment

Dignity Business Consulting is committed to protecting your privacy. All personal information provided will be handled with care, and as an entity operating within Arizona, we adhere to all applicable laws and regulations pertaining to data protection — this includes GDPR compliance. We are committed to keeping data safe for users worldwide.

Data Collection, Processing, and Storage

We collect, store, and process information you provide directly (for example when logging in or completing a form) and information collected automatically (via cookies, log files, or web beacons). We use this data to operate, improve, and personalize our services. Processing may include gathering, recording, organizing, storing, or altering your information as required to provide our services.

Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

We may collect PII during registration and transactions to uniquely identify you. This may include:

  • Email address and contact number
  • Profile details you choose to provide
  • Transaction-related details necessary to fulfill services

Your profile settings give you control over what is made public. Data stored in your account is protected by access controls and encrypted networks, and is available only to authorized personnel. Public postings you make on our platform may be used for marketing or other purposes; however, we cannot control how third parties use information you make public.

Third-Party Information Processors and Service Providers

We partner with trusted providers to deliver our services. These include:

  • Cloud storage providers that securely store your data
  • Payment processors and fulfillment companies that manage transactions and deliver goods (we do not have access to their full payment data)
  • Analytics/advertising partners such as Google, YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook. Their collection and use of information are governed by their own policies.
Technical Data Collection

To provide a high-quality service, we collect technical information such as:

  • IP address and device/browser details
  • Usage patterns and interaction data
  • Cookies to maintain sessions, personalize content, and facilitate transactions
  • Web beacons that help monitor email delivery and engagement
User Consent and Self-Service Control

By using our services, you acknowledge and consent to the collection and use of your data as described in this Policy. Through your account settings, you can access, modify, or delete certain personal information. You may manage cookies and tracking technologies via your browser; restricting data sharing may affect some features. Parents/guardians should supervise children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from such children without verified parental consent.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for legal, business, and auditing purposes. Certain account data may be retained after account closure when required for legitimate business or regulatory compliance.

Security and Disclosure

We employ administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction. While no system is completely secure, our protocols are reviewed regularly and access is restricted to authorized personnel.

Our Data Protection Officers

Our Data Protection Officers oversee our data protection strategy. If you have questions or concerns about how your data is handled, please contact us at support@dignitybusinessconsulting.com.

Access Requests

You have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you where applicable. To exercise these rights, email support@dignitybusinessconsulting.com.

Right to Oppose

In certain cases, you may object to our use of your data for marketing purposes. To exercise this right, email support@dignitybusinessconsulting.com.

International Transmission of Data

Your personal data may be processed and transferred across countries. We ensure all such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws so your privacy remains protected.

Policy Review and Modification

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Your continued use of our services after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
